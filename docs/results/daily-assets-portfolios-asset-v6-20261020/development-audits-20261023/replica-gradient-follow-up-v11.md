# Replica-gradient follow-up under V11

This is a development update, not a replacement for the completed full-panel result. The official V11 record remains the unchanged 167-recipe endpoint at **Q = 0.5442026571**, versus fixed WR **Q = 0.6510567494**, or **16.412408% lower standardized CRPS** over all 320,017 matched requests. The historical matched pairs contain 59,906 requests per model at 1,000 final paths and 260,111 at 240, with the same count within every pair. This follow-up adds **zero** full-panel catalogue entries and does not imply production promotion.

## Uniform-lead development update — 2026-10-23

On the same matched 17,395-request, 1,264-series representative-mass design, uniform-lead 2P has **Q = 0.5683632017**, versus fixed WR **Q = 0.6971753165**: **18.476287% lower standardized CRPS**. Its point estimate is only **0.087416% lower** than the preceding endpoint 2P estimate, 0.5688604761. Medium-bucket loss is **1.546196% higher**; short and long are lower. These are historical development estimates at prespecified internal seed 0, with 240 final paths and 315 source-bound reused requests per method. They do not replace the actual 320,017-request score or imply full-panel or production promotion.

The uniform learner samples a native lead inside each historical fold and estimates its population CRPS gradient divided by √(original fold H), while retaining the original-H joint generator. The fixed 41-recipe menu, request-local QP initialization, AdaGrad and pre-update averaging remain. This changes the internal learning target; external V11 stays fixed.

The small point gain is **not seed-robust** on a separate fixed 90-request diagnostic. With all three prespecified internal seeds reported, uniform 2P is 0.413716% lower than endpoint 2P at seed 0, **6.109578% higher** at seed 1, and 0.022543% higher at seed 2. This screen has 360 new method outputs over 180 context-seed pairs plus 180 retained seed 0 outputs; no best seed is selected. Its equal-three-bucket conditional summaries are separate from the 17,395-request representative-mass estimate. In particular, seed 1's short-bucket uniform loss is 2.425595% higher, not lower.

The earlier endpoint point estimate and learning contract remain below as dated history. The corrected crosscheck supersedes an earlier mismatched narrative without changing its preserved bytes, any forecasts, or the official score.

The candidate also has no all-horizon dominance: its short-bucket Q is 2.17766% higher than mapped-ratio. Neither full-panel nor production promotion follows.

The prior mapped-ratio pointer remains preserved as history: **Q = 0.5765574379**, or 17.30094% below fixed WR on the same representative design. That endpoint pointer changed the development incumbent only. Both pointer snapshots, the plan, source bindings, forecasts, and independent audit are in the private all-series recovery archive linked below.

The request-local learning rule is distinct from the final financial candidate bytes. For each dated request, let **P** be its number of eligible mapped-ratio folds. Initialize from that request's mapped-ratio QP; each stochastic step samples a fold by represented mass and a recipe by current simplex weight, then uses fresh independent full-bank **A** and one-recipe **B** replicas to form a terminal-CRPS/√H gradient. Tangent-projected Euclidean AdaGrad updates the simplex, and the reported weights average pre-update iterates. The `replica_2P` result uses exactly **2P** updates per request. The financial candidate source (SHA `d79c516a…a24ee850`) is different from the execution source (SHA `7f1c419e…6306138c`). The execution-equivalence ledger (SHA `1e77f732…f235838a`) binds 315 reused outputs under their legacy model ID and 17,080 new outputs under the current model ID. Full identifiers and hashes are listed separately in the JSON record.

## Rejections and execution diagnostics

- A 90-request single-path replica-gradient screen was independently audited and rejected for expansion. The Qs below are selected-cohort conditional diagnostics, not a full-panel headline.
- In that screen's equal-three-bucket conditional diagnostic, fixed WR was 0.917256 and mapped-ratio was 0.494107. Replica Qs were 0.525313 (P), 0.478004 (2P), and 0.481556 (4P); single-path Qs were 0.509705, 0.532310, and 0.491620. Thus single-path 2P and 4P were respectively 11.36% and 2.09% worse than their same-budget replica arms. Single-path P was 2.97% lower than replica P but remained 3.16% higher than mapped-ratio. These are conditional summaries on the selected 90 requests, not representative-mass estimates. All three buckets and the matched scope are recorded in the JSON.
- The 90-request full-support replica-gradient screen did not justify expansion: its 2P version was 3.09% worse than the matched 2P parent and its 4P version was 1.21% worse; oracle CPU use was also higher.
- The standalone latent-posterior extension was worse on its matched 90-request diagnostic: equal-three-bucket Q was 0.766140 versus 0.494107 for mapped-ratio, 55.06% higher. It was rejected for expansion. Its earlier three-case fit-support check had no aggregate score.
- Two later guard-only executor copies were rejected. G3 and G4 preserved all prescribed outputs exactly but were slower in every one of nine method×horizon comparisons: **7.20–18.14%** for G3 and **3.59–7.88%** for G4. Each study used 108 timed calls after 18 warmups, including nine own-reference qualification calls, on H=1/1,824/8,099. Balanced old/new orders and one active resident limited ordering effects; these were loaded local warm timings, not an uncontended or deployed-site claim. The existing G1/G2 executors remain in use.
- The guarded source-graph requested-budget run measured 36 executions after six warmups on three selected horizons. Median total wall seconds (and CPU seconds) for original full-4P → guarded requested-2P were **1.345 (1.169) → 0.204 (0.180)** at H=1, **1.291 (1.131) → 0.253 (0.222)** at H=1,824, and **1.536 (1.368) → 0.420 (0.361)** at H=8,099. Median paired wall ratios were 6.15×, 4.85×, and 3.66×. Both executors reported the same 2P financial result: the original also computed the unused trajectory through 4P, while the guarded executor stopped after the requested 2P. All six selected comparison outputs matched exactly in weights, paths, and CRPS. These local timings combine removal of unused work with source-graph caching, so they do not isolate graph-cache savings or establish a deployed, browser, or user-visible speed gain.

The studies reuse historical origins and do not establish fresh holdout performance. Full scopes, audit hashes, and recovery references are in the [JSON record](replica-gradient-follow-up-v11.json). Existing private recovery links retain earlier studies; receipt hashes also identify newer privately preserved evidence.

# Training-derived mean shrinkage

The working model uses coordinate-wise smooth shrinkage mu_a^2/(mu_a^2+C_aa), where C is the weighted batch covariance of mean uncertainty. Keep its covariance estimator, daily shocks, raw EWMA fade, SV-RMS scales, asset-level simulation and portfolio accounting. Change the mean treatment through a correlated normal empirical-Bayes approximation; do not choose shrinkage from held-out forecasts.

Given training mean mu and C, use mu | theta ~ N(theta,C) and theta ~ N(0,tau*C) on the nonzero-noise subspace. For rank r and E=mu' C^+ mu, marginal likelihood gives tau=max(0,E/r-1), hence retained mean fraction k=max(0,1-r/E). Preserve the observed mean in zero-noise directions. The eigenvalue tolerance is floating-point numerical rank, not a fitted statistical threshold. No allocation, ticker, asset class or horizon enters this fit.

Two frozen contrasts isolate the point mean from posterior contraction: empirical-Bayes center with unchanged covariance, and that center with Gaussian conditional covariance k*C. The latter is the plug-in conjugate posterior; it omits hyperparameter uncertainty. C is itself estimated and financial returns need not be Gaussian, so no exact posterior calibration, domination, independence or significance claim is made. Refit the existing training daily-CRPS component weight using the changed mean. All other financial laws and parent RNG namespaces remain unchanged. The parent control must replay exactly at100and1000paths.

Academic motivation: Xie, Kou and Brown (2012), https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/Brown_2013_SURE_Estimates_5.pdf, normal empirical-Bayes means and noise-aware shrinkage. Their independent heteroscedastic asymptotics are not guarantees for this correlated estimated-C plug-in model. George, Liang and Xu (2012), https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.5617, distinguishes shrinkage estimation from predictive-density results; their KL-risk results do not imply a CRPS improvement. The present correlated proportional-C marginal-likelihood solution is derived above and verified against direct training likelihood in tests.

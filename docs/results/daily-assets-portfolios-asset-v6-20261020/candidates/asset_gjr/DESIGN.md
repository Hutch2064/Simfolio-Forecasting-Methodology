# Joint-asset GJR filter prototype

One cohesive hybrid law: retain the current verified raw component, joint empirical row draws, smooth mean treatment, correlated Gaussian mean uncertainty, fixed equal mixture, constituent paths and portfolio accounting. Change only the filtered half to a per-constituent, training-only sign-asymmetric conditional variance law. Positive and negative shocks may have different coefficients. No asset/family/horizon routes, outcome-fitted weights, new score, clipping or path exclusion.

The mechanism is supported by Glosten, Jagannathan and Runkle (1993), [On the Relation between the Expected Value and the Volatility of the Nominal Excess Return on Stocks](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1993.tb05128.x). This is a variance-filter contrast, not a reproduction of that paper's full GARCH-in-mean specification or evidence that it must improve this panel.

Estimator functions are derived from the preserved earlier GJR prototype, recorded in source_derivation.json. It had training qualification before the asset-level change; no earlier full-panel GJR accuracy result justifies rejecting or promoting this joint-asset version. Three fixed training starts are used. The exact symmetric parent is a nested member, selected only when its training likelihood is better. Failed asymmetric optimizations are disclosed; they are not relabeled as successful fits. Positive/negative empirical innovation moments set the simulation intercept so unconditional variance targeting is preserved. Sufficient stationarity constraints are imposed without outcome calibration.

Four numerical tests check gradients, empirical variance targeting/stationarity, exact symmetric nesting and unchanged raw paths under partition and circular raw parents, and recovery of sign asymmetry with scale equivariance. A710-prefix training-only qualification is required before any forecast screen. Freeze the raw parent from the actual verified current best at admission, after the simpler candidate results are available. Passing training fits is not evidence of forecast accuracy; check credibility cheaply, then confirm at1,000paths before any official score.

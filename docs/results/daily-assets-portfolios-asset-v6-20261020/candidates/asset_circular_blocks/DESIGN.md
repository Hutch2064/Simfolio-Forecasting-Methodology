# Circular raw-block component contrast

This is one fixed joint-asset forecasting law, without asset-class, family or horizon routing. Everything in the verified hybrid remains unchanged except its raw block index stream. Draw each block length uniformly from 1 through min(training rows, forecast observations), draw its historical starting row uniformly from every training row, and copy consecutive rows modulo training length. Blocks are sampled with replacement. All constituents share those row indices; portfolio accounting stays inside each simulated path. The filtered GARCH component, mean regularization, correlated mean uncertainty, equal mixture, paths, score and data remain fixed.

Training-only mechanism evidence showed that restricting starts to blocks wholly inside the history changes endpoint-row exposure. Circular starts remove that edge exposure effect. The ordinary nonwrapping replacement contrast lost its 100-path screen; this is a distinct circular-boundary mechanism, not a retry with a new seed or an outcome-fitted block length. It has no presumed accuracy benefit and must earn advancement on matched forecasts.

The circular-boundary idea is supported by Politis and Romano, *A Circular Block-Resampling Procedure for Stationary Data* ([Stanford technical report](https://statistics.stanford.edu/technical-reports/circular-block-resampling-procedure-stationary-data)). Our uniformly bounded random block distribution is a research specification, not a claim to reproduce that paper exactly or its stationary-bootstrap geometric-length method. Historical financial returns need not be stationary; the paper does not prove forecasting superiority here.

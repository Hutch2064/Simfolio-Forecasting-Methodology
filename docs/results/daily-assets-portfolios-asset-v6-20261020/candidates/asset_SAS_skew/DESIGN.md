# Isolated innovation asymmetry

Academic basis: Jones and Pewsey (2009), Sinh-arcsinh distributions, https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/asp053. Their later explanation, https://rss.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/j.1740-9713.2019.01245.x, describes a Gaussian-containing skew/tail family. This study uses its delta=1 skew-only subfamily, not a heavy-tail power change or a claim that the literature guarantees improved return forecasts.

For Z standard normal, m=E[sqrt(1+Z^2)] and v=2-m^2, define X={Z+t(sqrt(1+Z^2)-m)}/sqrt(1+t^2*v), |t|<1. This is the centered, variance-standardized version of sinh(asinh(Z)+epsilon), t=tanh(epsilon). Mean zero and variance one follow analytically from symmetry. Each marginal transform is strictly increasing; the existing latent Gaussian copula is retained. Its implied Pearson correlations can change under nonlinear marginal transformations; they are not falsely claimed to be identical. The exact Gaussian endpoint t=0 is retained without extra arithmetic.

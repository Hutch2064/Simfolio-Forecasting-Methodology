# Training-estimated CRPS pooling

Six frozen laws cross the current moment-SV conditional RMS Gaussian filter or a Gaussian GARCH filter with raw circular blocks using GARCH fade, fitted EWMA fade or no volatility overlay. The fraction allocated to raw simulation is fitted separately from each actual training prefix, once for the entire joint asset model. There is no percentage grid, selected training split, outcome routing or forecast-horizon weight switch. Mean shrinkage, correlated mean uncertainty and joint asset/in-path portfolio accounting remain the prior model's methods.

For each standardized constituent and every observed training day, form a leave-one-out empirical raw return distribution with that day's predicted raw scale and a Gaussian filtered marginal with its predicted scale. Optimize the sum of conditional one-day marginal CRPS across all constituents and training days. Both share the training-shrunk mean. Uncertainty in the population drift is conditioned out for this daily innovation-fitting criterion; the unchanged correlated drift draw remains in actual forecasts. Raw source rows exclude the evaluated observation, but marginal/filter/mean parameters are fitted to the entire available training prefix. This is a training minimum-score estimator, **not** genuinely out-of-fit prequential validation. No observation after the actual forecast origin enters. The outer matched panel score alone determines whether the resulting forecasting procedure improves.

For empirical A and Gaussian B, CRPS(w A+(1-w)B,y)=c+l w+q w^2. With a=E|A-A'|/2, b=E|B-B'|/2 and d=E|A-B|, c=E|B-y|-b, l=E|A-y|-E|B-y|+2b-d, q=d-a-b>=0. The optimizer is the constrained quadratic minimum on[0,1]. If the laws are indistinguishable, choose the simpler raw endpoint. Terms involving empirical observations are exactly leave-one-out, including their pairwise self-distance. Gaussian absolute expectations are analytic. Cross-distances use piecewise-linear interpolation with a derived relative error bound of1e-6; this is a numerical precision setting, not a financial allocation. Tests compare brute-force exact small-history expressions, an independent scalar minimizer, finer precision and the recorded objective-error bound.

All finite ensembles contain exactly the declared number of paths, with no weighted CRPS. Allocate the nearest even number of paths to the learned raw fraction so the existing antithetic mean draws remain valid; the realized fraction differs from the fitted fraction by at most1/N. A forced half-weight GARCH-fade/SV-RMS control must reproduce the existing working model bit for bit at100 and1000paths. Real learned weights remain unchanged after inspecting outer development, reserved or broad outcomes.

Primary methodological motivation: Gneiting and Raftery (2007), Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction, and Estimation, https://sites.stat.washington.edu/people/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf ; Geweke and Amisano, Optimal Prediction Pools, https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/pdf/scpwps/ecbwp1017.pdf . This adapter uses training marginal CRPS rather than their complete predictive-density estimation procedure and does not claim an exact paper replication or guarantee improved multi-horizon forecasts.

This is a controlled training-estimation contrast around the working model. It is not a change to the frozen baseline, panel, scoring, development/reserved samples, final full-evaluation gate or original stopping condition. Exploratory low-path tests precede matched1000-path checks. These adaptively reused cases do not establish independence or significance. Full-panel superiority requires the final retained suite after the evidence-based stopping review.

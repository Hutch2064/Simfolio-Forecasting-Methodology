# Training-regularized conditional mean in the asset-level FHS half

Motivation: the training-only546-context lag-one diagnostic finds meaningful return association in some constituents, including broad equity portfolios. Overlapping contexts are not independent; staleness and calendars may generate association. The earlier scalar MA1 law failed reserved tests, so this is a cautious new joint-asset AR1 mechanism test, not a claim that all autoregressions help.

Keep the verified GARCH-fade raw circular half bitwise unchanged. In the filtered half estimate one AR1 slope per asset by demeaned OLS. Shrink its coefficient by phi^2/(phi^2+HACse^2); use the same fixed training-only Bartlett bandwidth floor(n^(1/3)) as the mechanism diagnostic, then enforce stationary coefficient support. Fit the existing variance-targeted GARCH estimator on the resulting innovations. Use all training rows, initializing the first lag at the training mean. Historical innovations are centered and normalized by the existing fitter and sampled as complete joint rows.

Within each scenario, r_t = m + phi*(r_(t-1)-m) + sigma_t*z_t, where m is the unchanged smooth training point mean plus jointly correlated Gaussian batch-mean uncertainty. Initialize r_(t-1) at the actual last training return; future uncertainty about m is fixed within the path. The conditional location effect fades geometrically without a horizon cutoff. The GARCH recursion uses innovation shocks, not total AR returns. Long-run innovation variance is fitted on innovations; no assertion is made that finite-sample unconditional asset variance is exactly unchanged. Per-scenario asset holdings and portfolio accounting remain inside the path.

When the coefficient is zero, reuse the incumbent raw GARCH fit and recover its entire law exactly. No held-out calibration, alternative horizon score, class route, parameter grid, path clipping or loss exclusion is allowed. The extra innovation fit is an explicit compute cost; score remains primary. Qualification and matched low-count testing precede1000-path development, reserved, broad or full admission.

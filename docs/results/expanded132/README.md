# Expanded 132-series research protocol

The new [protocol resource](../../../src/simfolio_forecasting_methodology/resources/protocols/portfolio80_asset52_equal_weight_v1.yaml) preserves the exact frozen 80-portfolio experiment and adds the sorted union of its 52 actual tickers as 100% single-asset holdings. It defines a separate score: **0.5 × original80 mean CRPS + 0.5 × single52 mean CRPS**. This gives each block equal weight; averaging all 132 series would answer a different question. Original80 scores and legacy ranking semantics remain unchanged.

Both blocks use the same price-derived logs, 1979-12-31 through 2026-05-13 (11,687 rows), 48 rolling origins plus three temporal holdouts per series, 240 paths, and every eligible daily horizon through 8,766 days. The original block has 4,080 tasks and 701,280 cells; single52 has 2,652 tasks and 455,832 cells. CRPS targets terminal log returns with the exact empirical n-squared pairwise denominator. Origin losses are averaged within each series/horizon cell, then cells within each block. Missing/nonfinite vectors or incomplete denominators fail closed.

The [machine-readable report](score-report.json) binds the protocol, complete result files, exact manifests, ranked full-cell arrays and independent audit receipts by SHA-256. Compressed manifests decompress to the original JSON bytes, preserving both their file hashes and canonical manifest hashes. Original80 incumbent evidence is referenced in its existing retained artifact, without copying or modifying it. Partial control cell checksums remain bound to their result receipts; their bulk checkpoints remain with the campaign owner.

The M261 pool improves this expanded score by 2.9688769373% relative to M261. It concatenates 120 complete paths from each component, without averaging returns, parameters or scores. The 120-node component laws differ from standalone 240-path ensembles. This is a new mixture law. No ticker gate, fitted OOS weight or additional rejoining/fees is introduced. The EWMA component's exact default branch uses a scalar shrunken mean: `posterior_mu_draws=False` and `posterior_sd_scale=0`. Its complementarity includes mean shrinkage, residual blocks and absolute EWMA volatility; no isolated causal attribution of the score gain has been established.

The new stationary-OU pool ranks second on the expanded score, behind the M261 pool and ahead of standalone M261. It concatenates 120 complete stationary-OU residual-volatility paths and 120 Bayesian EWMA-SBB paths at execution revision `3aee75cab77d152b3311acf5da061dc07d339082`. Its [combined independent audit](ou-pool-independent-audit.json) covers all 6,732 tasks and 1,157,112 cells; both block audits independently reproduce every cell and count and verify all 27,132 accepted OU fit records succeeded. Existing overflow warnings from optimizer trial evaluations are preserved; accepted fits and scored loss vectors passed validation. Observed block elapsed times of 97.857 seconds (original80) and 38.499 seconds (single52) are separate-run local diagnostics, without a matched speedup claim.

The original M261 pool's [loss decomposition](pool-loss-decomposition.json) attributes about 75% of its aggregate gain to horizons 5,041–8,766 days. All horizons remain included. It improves 21 of 52 individual assets and worsens 31, including SPYSIM; this is an aggregate improvement rather than a universal asset improvement. Removing any one asset or original portfolio leaves the aggregate gain positive. The unchanged [decomposition helper](reproduction/decompose_pool_losses.py) and receipt preserve exact source score reductions, including approximately 1e-16 differences from alternate floating-point reduction orders. These descriptive results motivate further investigation and do not identify a causal mechanism.

The [long-CAGR evidence](long-cagr/audit-receipt.json) retains all 224 terminal-path archives and case records: 52 assets and four portfolios covering all rebalance frequencies, two models, and 50/100-year horizons. It separately reports median path CAGR, mean path CAGR and CAGR of mean ending NAV. These are prospective coherence diagnostics beyond available held-out history, **not OOS scores**. Ratios below 0.5/0.75 times positive historical CAGR are descriptive diagnostics, never fitting constraints. Historical CAGR is a reference rather than a forecast target. The unchanged [campaign script](reproduction/long_cagr_audit.py) records its original sibling-directory assumptions.

The [matched local runtime attachment](matched-pool-runtime.json) contains four bounded cases, 48 alternating samples, 240 paths and one thread with path caches cleared. It distinguishes cold fit (compiler already warm) and warm fit, and retains 27-resample paired percentile-bootstrap intervals and exact task bindings. Some long cases improve while singleton short/medium cases can worsen. These measurements compare the original M261 pool with M261 and support only the measured local cases; they establish neither a global runtime win nor production/browser click-to-visible performance. No production change or release is implied by this research publication.

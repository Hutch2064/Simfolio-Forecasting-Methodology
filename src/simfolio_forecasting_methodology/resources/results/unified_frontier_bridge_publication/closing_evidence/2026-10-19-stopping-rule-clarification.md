# Addendum: score-independent stopping rule — 2026-10-19

This addendum clarifies the stopping paragraph in `2026-10-19-post-iid-interaction-stopping-assessment.md`; that original report is unchanged. A first result that beats M261 on both canonical panels is a finalist milestone, not by itself permission to stop methodology work. After the full reserve × corrected-continuous-node result is independently reviewed, perform the remaining-mechanism audit regardless of whether its score wins or loses. Close this bounded local search only if that audit finds no further evidence-supported single mechanism or interaction. This is an evidence-based stopping boundary, not a fixed “last candidate” cap; a specific saved diagnostic can justify reopening with one predeclared both-panel contrast.

The present audit identifies one active fixed interaction: the retained-rough reserve with continuous-node variance correction. Its six-case actual pilot and independent saved-output review passed (`480a303`); the full run `conditional_rough_corrected_nodes/full-021da156f7455bce` is in progress, so no final panel score or admission is claimed here. The independently completed IID scale law and full-total-mean law do not support automatic stacking: IID changes marginal tails and cross-asset dependence while omitting future SV-state evolution, and it already uses the corrected-node base; the full-total-mean correction includes the rough component that the retained-rough path already simulates. Neither the score sign of the active interaction nor a joint win alone establishes another model.

One conditional follow-up remains auditable, rather than pre-authorized: if saved path-level diagnostics from the completed reserve × node study isolate a material residual specifically attributable to the absent conventional-scale first moment, a conventional-only compensation on that retained-rough path could support one frozen both-panel contrast. Existing aggregate location/width differences and the panel-opposite total-mean result do not alone identify that residual. If the diagnostics do not isolate it, there is no current source-supported second interaction. Descriptive portfolio or horizon heterogeneity still does not authorize routing, a strength grid, or fitted cutoffs.

Even after any methodology search is closed, the two development panels do not constitute independent validation. A finalist still needs prespecified paired seed sensitivity without seed selection, a new frozen chronological holdout for generalization claims, and separate matched single-asset/multiasset timing evidence if speed is claimed.

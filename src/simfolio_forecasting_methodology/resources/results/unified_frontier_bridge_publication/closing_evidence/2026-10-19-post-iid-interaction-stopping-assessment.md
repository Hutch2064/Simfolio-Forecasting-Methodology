## Remaining evidence-supported branches

After the one reserve × corrected-node contrast, the current saved evidence supports **no automatic second forecast variant**. The remaining potentially relevant mechanisms have already been probed, but their results do not support stacking them:

- The IID law is complete and close to M261 on 80, but still misses M261 there. It changes both asset marginal tails and cross-asset dependence and draws no future SV states; it is not a pure serial-clustering experiment. Its node choices already match the selected corrected-node base, so a further node-by-IID grid has no separate support.
- The complete analytic total-mean law scores `0.5973358294 / 0.2315534441` on 52/80. Relative to the completed total-covariance law it worsens 52 by about 0.26% and improves 80 by about 0.36%, while still missing M261 on 80. That law draws no future SV paths. Adding its full `b*expm1(-(vc+vr)/8)` correction on top of the actual-rough reserve would reuse the rough first-moment effect already represented by simulated rough states; it is not a justified plug-in interaction.
- A later, single conventional-mean companion to the retained-rough reserve could be reconsidered only if the fixed interaction’s saved path-level diagnostics reveal a location residual specifically aligned with a material missing conventional-scale first moment. It would need a formula that integrates only the absent conventional component conditional on the retained rough path, then one frozen both-panel comparison. The present small, panel-opposite analytic total-mean effect and long-growth summaries do not meet that condition.

If the corrected-node reserve beats M261 on both panels, end local methodology search for this bounded panel and move to finalist verification. If it misses either panel, do not fill a grid or declare every future mechanism exhausted: stop this current branch and require a new source-derived or saved-data diagnostic that identifies one specific residual before admitting another one-cell contrast. Existing horizon and portfolio heterogeneity are descriptive, not authorization for routing or fitted cutoffs.

## Validation gates are separate from model research

No controlled timing result exists for IID or the retained-rough corrected-node candidate; the six-case pilot's stage times are not matched benchmark evidence. Selected RMS timings do not transfer to laws that reintroduce rough fitting/state evolution. A finalist needs the existing matched fresh/cached protocol with both single-asset and actual multiasset contexts, stage-separated timing, and no website-latency claim.

No independent-seed robustness evidence exists. The IID supplemental seed study is marked ineligible/blocked and unrun, not a negative model result. After one fixed finalist is selected by complete-panel scores, use a prespecified paired seed sensitivity check against M261 without seed selection. Because both development panels have informed methodology choices, a seed rerun is not post-search validation; a generalization claim needs a new frozen chronological holdout.
